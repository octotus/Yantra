Yantra celestial chart data
==========================

Star positions and visual magnitudes
-----------------------------------
The 5,044 records in celestial/stars.tsv are converted from stars.6.json in
Olaf Frohn's d3-celestial project:
https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial
https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial/blob/master/data/stars.6.json

Copyright (c) 2015, Olaf Frohn. Distributed under the BSD 3-Clause license;
the full notice is included in D3-CELESTIAL-BSD.txt.

d3-celestial identifies its stellar data source as:
XHIP: An Extended Hipparcos Compilation, E. Anderson and C. Francis (2012),
Astronomy Letters 38, 331; VizieR catalogue V/137D.
https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/V/137D

Yantra converts the GeoJSON records to TSV, normalizes right ascension to
0..360 degrees, and retains HIP identifiers, coordinates and magnitudes.

Stellar patterns
----------------
The 12 rashi context patterns are adapted from Stellarium's Western sky
culture. Text and data authors: Stellarium's team. Upstream license: CC BY-SA.
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium-skycultures/tree/master/western

The 27 nakshatra patterns are adapted from Stellarium's Indian sky culture.
Authors: Tanmoy Saha, Vishvas Vasuki, contributors from the sanskrit-coders
community; reworked by Susanne M Hoffmann. Upstream license: CC BY-SA.
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium-skycultures/tree/master/indian

The upstream descriptions specify CC BY-SA without a version. These adapted
pattern data retain that attribution and share-alike designation; they are
separate from Yantra's AGPL application source. License overview:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/

Changes: only the twelve zodiac constellations and first 27 lunar stations
are retained; line chains become individual edges; zero-length edges are
removed while retaining single-star patterns. UI names use Yantra's catalogue.
No Stellarium translations or constellation boundaries are used.

Zodiac illustration outlines
----------------------------
The twelve zodiac illustrations are derived from Stellarium's Western sky
culture artwork by Johan Meuris. Upstream license: Free Art License 1.3.
https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium-skycultures/tree/master/western
https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/

Yantra converts the grayscale WebP artwork into transparent gold edge maps and
uses the upstream three-star HIP anchors to align each outline with the fixed
J2000 chart. The derived PNG artwork remains under the Free Art License 1.3.
The source revision and input hashes are recorded in
celestial/zodiac_art_source_hashes.json. Artwork was retrieved on 2026-10-08.

Uttara Bhadrapada: corrected upstream HIP 667 to HIP 677 (Alpheratz), paired
with HIP 1067 (Algenib), following Susanne M Hoffmann and Youla Azkarrula:
https://ase.exopla.net/index.php/Uttara_Bhadrapada

Source files were retrieved on 2026-09-09. Their SHA-256 fingerprints are
recorded in celestial/source_hashes.json. These notices do not imply endorsement.
